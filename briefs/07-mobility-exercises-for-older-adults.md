## Search Intent

Two audiences: older adults themselves, and adult children looking for
something for a parent. Both want safe, specific exercises and some reassurance
that they're appropriate.

## Angle

Organize the post around the tasks independence depends on rather than body
parts: getting down to and up from the floor, rising from a chair, stairs,
reaching overhead and behind, turning to look, and balance. Each task gets the
joints and drills that support it. Respectful tone: no "elderly" in headings,
no talking down.

## Outline

1. **Intro (no heading).** Frame mobility as independence. Link
   `/what-does-mobility-mean-we-asked-the-pros` (its "Mobility Is
   Independence" section).
2. **## Before You Start.** Clearance from a doctor if there's a heart
   condition, recent surgery, osteoporosis, or a fall history. Have a chair or
   counter nearby. Effort and pain guidelines.
3. **## Mobility Exercises for Seniors, by Task.** `###` per task, two drills
   each with dose and an easier and harder version:
   - Getting up from the floor (a staged floor transfer)
   - Rising from a chair (sit-to-stand, then slower or from a lower seat)
   - Stairs (step-ups, ankle work; link the ankle post)
   - Reaching overhead and behind (seated shoulder CARs, wall slides)
   - Turning to look over a shoulder (seated thoracic rotation)
   - Balance (supported single-leg stance, heel-to-toe walking)
4. **## If Mobility Is Already Limited.** Seated versions of the key drills
   for people who can't stand safely for long.
5. **## How Often.** Daily short sessions for mobility; cite the WHO (or CDC)
   recommendation for older adults on balance and strength work across the
   week. State the guideline exactly as the source does.
6. **## Why Strength Belongs in the Same Plan.** Short: mobility without
   strength doesn't protect against falls. Link
   `/mobility-as-capacity-not-a-category` and brief 09's slug once live.
7. **## When to Stop and Call Someone.** Dizziness, chest pain, new joint
   swelling, a fall.
8. **## References**
