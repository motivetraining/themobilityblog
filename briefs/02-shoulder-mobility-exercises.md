## Search Intent

Practical. People want a list of exercises they can do today, ideally a
routine. Many have a specific goal: overhead pressing, pull-ups, a front rack,
or simply reaching overhead without the low back arching.

## Angle

Most shoulder lists are stretches and band pull-aparts. This post separates
the shoulder into its parts (the glenohumeral joint and the scapula, plus the
thoracic spine it sits on) and gives drills that build strength at end range,
so the range survives contact with a barbell.

## Outline

1. **Intro (no heading).** Scene: someone stretches their lats and pecs for
   months, and their overhead position still collapses into a low back arch.
2. **## Test Before You Train.** Two or three quick self-tests with no
   equipment: back-to-the-wall overhead reach (flexion), shoulder external and
   internal rotation with the elbow at 90 degrees, and a shoulder CAR. What a
   limit looks like and what compensation looks like.
3. **## Why Shoulders Get Stuck.** Brief: the scapula has to move for the arm
   to go overhead; a stiff thoracic spine limits it (link brief 03's slug once
   live); passive range without strength at the end gets lost.
4. **## The Exercises.** Eight drills, each a `###` with setup, movement,
   fault, dose. Suggested list (swap if something better fits):
   1. Shoulder CARs
   2. Scapular CARs
   3. Wall slides with a lift-off (hands come off the wall at the top)
   4. Prone or standing flexion lift-offs (end-range overhead strength)
   5. External rotation PAILs and RAILs (doorframe or floor setup)
   6. Internal rotation end-range isometrics (sleeper-style position, gently)
   7. Thoracic extension over a foam roller paired with an overhead reach
   8. A loaded finisher: bottoms-up kettlebell press or overhead carry
5. **## A 15-Minute Shoulder Mobility Routine.** A table or numbered list
   sequencing the drills, with total time, for three to four sessions a week.
6. **## Stretching vs Strengthening the Shoulder.** Short. Stretching can add
   passive range; strength at end range is what keeps it. Link
   `/flexibility-is-range-mobility-is-what-you-can-do-with-it` and
   `/isometrics-infinite-tension-infinite-potential`.
7. **## When to Get It Checked.** Pinching at the top of the shoulder when
   reaching overhead, night pain, weakness after a fall, a history of
   dislocation or a shoulder that feels like it slips (these people need
   stability work and a clinician, not more range; link
   `/when-stretching-makes-pain-worse-how-to-spot-the-red-flags`).
8. **## References**
