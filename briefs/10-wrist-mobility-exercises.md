## Search Intent

People whose wrists hurt or block them in a specific position: push-ups,
planks, front rack, handstands, yoga. They want exercises and to know if their
wrists are the actual problem.

## Angle

Wrist extension under load is one of the most demanding positions in
training, and most people never train it. Also: a bad front rack is often a
shoulder, lat, or thoracic problem showing up at the wrist. The post helps
readers tell which.

## Outline

1. **Intro (no heading).** Scene: push-ups or a front squat where the wrist
   aches before the target muscles do.
2. **## What the Wrist Has to Do.** Short: flexion, extension, radial and
   ulnar deviation at the wrist; pronation and supination at the forearm.
   Which positions demand the most extension. Verify any normal-range figures.
3. **## Test Your Wrist Extension.** Quadruped or wall test, prayer position,
   comparison of active vs passive extension. What "limited wrist mobility"
   looks like.
4. **## Wrist Mobility Exercises.** `###` each with dose: wrist CARs (link
   `/what-cars-are-actually-for`); quadruped wrist rocks with finger
   lift-offs; wrist extension PAILs and RAILs (link brief 04); forearm
   pronation and supination with a light hammer or dumbbell; end-range
   extension isometrics; loaded progression (plank and push-up positions,
   then handstand prep against a wall).
5. **## Front Squat Wrist Mobility.** Why the front rack is often limited by
   shoulder external rotation, lat length, or thoracic extension rather than
   the wrist. Quick checks and rack alternatives (fingertip grip, wider grip,
   straps). Link brief 02 and brief 03.
6. **## A 5-Minute Wrist Routine.** Before push-ups, handstands, or front
   squats.
7. **## When Wrist Pain Needs a Clinician.** Pain on the pinky side of the
   wrist (verify how to describe ulnar-sided pain accurately), clicking with
   pain, numbness or tingling in the fingers, swelling, pain after a fall.
