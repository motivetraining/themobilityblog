## Search Intent

How-to. People want to know how to sit in the position, what they should
feel, and whether they're doing it right. Many have seen it on social media
and can't get into it comfortably.

## Angle

Most guides treat 90/90 as a passive stretch you sit in. The site's angle: it
trains two different rotations at once (external rotation in the front hip,
internal rotation in the back hip), and sitting in it passively wastes most of
its value. The post teaches the position, then shows how to make it active.

## Outline

1. **Intro (no heading).** The common scene: someone sits in 90/90, leans
   onto their hands, back hip floating, and calls it done. Say what the
   position is in one sentence within the first 100 words.
2. **## How to Set Up the 90/90 Hip Stretch.** Numbered steps: both knees
   bent to roughly 90 degrees, front shin across the body, back shin to the
   side, torso tall, both sit bones heading toward the floor. What to feel and
   where. Dose for the passive version.
3. **## What Each Leg Is Doing.** Front hip in external rotation, back hip in
   internal rotation. Why the back hip is usually the limiter. Link
   `/hip-internal-rotation-the-range-most-people-lose`.
4. **## The Faults That Waste It.** Leaning back onto the hands, the back hip
   lifting, rounding forward from the low back instead of hinging at the hip,
   forcing the knee. One short paragraph each with the fix.
5. **## If You Can't Get Into It Yet.** Regressions: hands behind for support,
   sitting on a yoga block or a low box, narrowing the knee angle, working one
   side at a time. Address "90 90 hip stretch for beginners" here.
6. **## Making It Active.** The core of the post. Progressions in order: tall
   torso with hands off the floor; hip-hinge forward over the front shin;
   front-leg and back-leg lift-offs (lifting the foot or knee while holding the
   position); PAILs and RAILs in the 90/90 (short version, link brief 04's slug
   once live, Motive's PAILs/RAILs guide until then); transitions side to side
   without hands. Sets, reps, holds for each.
7. **## Where the 90/90 Fits in a Week.** How often, how long, before or after
   training. Link `/why-mobility-doesnt-stick` for frequency and
   `/how-do-you-structure-mobility-into-your-workout-routine`.
8. **## When to Stop.** Knee pain (often from forcing the shin flat or
   twisting at the knee instead of rotating at the hip), pinching at the front
   of the hip, sharp pain. Mention that some hips have a bony limit and won't
   get there, and that's fine.
