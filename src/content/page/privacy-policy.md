This policy explains what information The Mobility Blog collects from visitors and how it's used.

## What We Collect

This site uses basic hosting analytics (provided by Vercel) to understand traffic, such as which pages are viewed and general visitor location and device type. This is aggregate, non-identifying data used to see what content is working, not to track individuals.

We do not use tracking cookies, run ads, or sell visitor data.

## Contact Forms and Email

If you email us directly (for example, through the [Write With Us](/write-with-us) page), we receive whatever you choose to send us — your email address, name, and message content. We use this only to respond to you and don't share it with third parties.

## External Links

Posts on this site sometimes link to external sites, including [Motive Training](https://www.movewithpurpose.com/) and the Motive Mobility community on Circle. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this policy doesn't cover.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. Check back here for the current version.
