The Mobility Blog is a place to learn from industry experts on mobility training, stretching, coaching, and the principles that shape better movement.

Some posts are technical, some are philosophical, but the goal stays the same: help you move better, one joint at a time.

[Brian Murray](https://www.movewithpurpose.com/meet-the-founder) started The Mobility Blog to create a home for writing, editing, and collaborating on useful, honest work in the fitness and mobility space.
